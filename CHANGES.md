2026-05-09 — feat(quality): human-feel pass on Cortex prompt — 16-rule rewrite of `COPYWRITING_RULES`, expanded `AVOID` list, tightened `STRUCTURE` and `SEGMENT_INDIVIDUAL`. Driven by audit of 5 fresh test outputs that all read as AI-slop despite the prior thinking-first changes. Root causes identified: (1) fabricated stats in every post (40%, 60–70%, 75%, 68%, 42% — all invented by the model), (2) "I keep seeing" became a universal opener crutch, (3) tacked-on standalone IMPERFECTION line ("I'm still figuring out X"), (4) "X isn't Y. It's Z." reveal closer in every post (the most overused LinkedIn AI pattern), (5) trailing hashtags, (6) identical skeleton across all 5 posts, (7) plural abstractions ("founders," "marketers," "teams") instead of specific anonymized subjects. New rules: STAT INTEGRITY (hard ban on invented numbers — only research-passed stats allowed); SPECIFICITY OVER ABSTRACTION (mandate one anonymized subject with concrete attribute per post); BAN "I keep seeing" / "I've been seeing" as opener anchor; BAN "X isn't Y. It's Z." closer; BAN trailing hashtags entirely (was 0–3, now 0); INLINE DOUBT ONLY (no labeled "I'm still figuring out" paragraphs); ROTATE THE CLOSE (5 options: question / flat statement / admission / offer / no CTA); SHOW WORK DON'T NUMBER LESSONS; PLAIN VERBS list (use/find/push/grow vs leverage/unlock/drive/scale); FRAGMENTS ALLOWED (max 2, never 3 in a row); ASYMMETRIC STRUCTURE; ONE UNCOMFORTABLE TRUTH per post; expanded AVOID with fake-pivot tells ("Here's what's actually happening" / "Here's the thing"), tricolons of fragments, numbered lessons. `STRUCTURE` CTA section rewritten to mandate rotation; `SEGMENT_INDIVIDUAL` removed "I keep seeing..." as the safe-anchor fallback (replaced with "specific recent moment" requirement). Synced to `src/lib/ai/neel-prompt-sections.ts`. No TS/architecture changes.
