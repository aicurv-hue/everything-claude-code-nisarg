Your ideal prospect just shortlisted 5 vendors. You're one of them. They haven't contacted anyone yet — including you.

This is the silent evaluation era, and it's quietly breaking traditional B2B sales playbooks.

Here's what's actually changed: buyers now complete a significant chunk of their decision-making process before a single sales call happens. They compare pricing pages, read G2 reviews, watch competitor demos on YouTube, and build internal scorecards — all without raising their hand.

On content: blog posts that explain what your product does are largely wasted effort at the evaluation stage. Content that helps them build their internal business case will outperform content that just introduces your solution.

On demos: this one stings a little. The demo is no longer a discovery call dressed up with a screen share. Treat every demo as a validation call. Lead with differentiation. Assume they've done the homework.

The sales cycle feels longer because the buyer's journey started earlier, not because buyers are slower. The work that used to happen on a call now happens in private, on their timeline.

If your pipeline motion doesn't account for that gap, you're showing up late to a conversation that's already halfway over.
