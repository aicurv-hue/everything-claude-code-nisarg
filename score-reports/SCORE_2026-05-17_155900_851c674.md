Most AI ghostwriters produce posts that sound like AI ghostwriters. Here's what actually breaks that pattern.

The problem isn't laziness. It's architecture.

Third, hook diversity. The same opener formula, post after post, trains your audience to scroll past you. Rotating the structure keeps them reading.

The uncomfortable truth: most people using AI content tools can feel when a post sounds like theirs. They just publish it anyway because it's fast.

That's the real problem worth solving.
